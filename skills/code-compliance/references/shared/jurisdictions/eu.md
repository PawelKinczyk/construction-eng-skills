# Jurisdiction: European Union

Status: v0.1, reviewed 2026-10. Applies together with a member state file (for example `pl.md`).

EU law sets product rules and minimum requirements. Building design rules (fire safety, structural safety, energy limits) are mostly national. Always use the member state file for design limits.

## Legal hierarchy

1. **Regulations**: apply directly in all member states.
2. **Directives**: apply through national law. Cite the national act for design work; cite the directive for background.
3. **Harmonised standards (hEN)**: listed in the Official Journal. They give presumption of conformity for products.
4. **Other EN standards**: voluntary unless national law references them.

## Official legal sources

**Law only from official government sources.** Use only the domains below to read, check or cite law, regulations and official interpretations. Never use news sites, blogs, law firm articles, training providers or vendor pages as a legal source. When a web search tool supports a domain filter, set it to these domains. If the official source cannot be reached, mark the item "to verify".

Allowed domains: `europa.eu` and its subdomains (official EU institutions). For national law, use the member state file.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| EU law, consolidated texts | EUR-Lex, eur-lex.europa.eu |
| Harmonised standards lists (OJ references) | European Commission, single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu |
| Commission guidance (CPR, ecodesign, EPBD) | commission.europa.eu, energy.ec.europa.eu |
| Product energy label database | EPREL, eprel.ec.europa.eu |

## Permits and approvals

There is no EU building permit. Permits are national (see the member state file).
EU-level items that affect a project:
- CE marking and DoP for construction products.
- CE marking and declarations of conformity for equipment (LVD, EMC, machinery, PED).
- F-gas certification of installers and leak checks.
- Energy performance certificate (national form, based on EPBD).

## Price sources

No EU-wide unit rate database. Use the member state file.

## Units and conventions

- SI units (ISO 80000).
- Dates in documents: national format; ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD) for data and file names.
- Currency: EUR or national currency.
- Contract forms often used internationally: FIDIC (Red, Yellow, Silver books), NEC in some countries.
