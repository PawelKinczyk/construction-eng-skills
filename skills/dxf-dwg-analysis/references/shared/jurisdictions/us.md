## Official legal sources

**Law only from official government sources.** Use only the domains below to read, check or cite law, regulations and official interpretations. Never use news sites, blogs, law firm articles, training providers or vendor pages as a legal source. When a web search tool supports a domain filter, set it to these domains. If the official source cannot be reached, mark the item "to verify".

Allowed domains: `.gov` (federal, state and city government). Model codes (ICC, NFPA) become law only through state or local adoption. Check the adopting act and its edition on the state or city `.gov` site.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Federal regulations | ecfr.gov, federalregister.gov |
| Federal accessibility (ADA standards) | ada.gov, access-board.gov |
| Federal energy rules and code status | energy.gov, energycodes.gov |
| State and local code adoption | state and city `.gov` sites (building department, legislature) |
